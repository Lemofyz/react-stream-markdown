## ⚠️ Read first: how this was researched

- **No official page was opened directly.** This cloud environment's network policy blocks direct page access (WebFetch fails with DNS errors, the same as in the earlier research). Every finding comes from **web-search result summaries**. Text in quotes is wording that appeared in those summaries, and **has not been checked against the live page**.
- Each row names its source type: **[official]** means the summary came from the company's own domain (careers.microsoft.com, citadel.com, janestreet.com, metacareers.com, SpaceX's Greenhouse board, jobs.uber.com). **[third-party]** means an aggregator, blog or university career office. Third-party sources often disagree with each other and go stale.
- "Date checked" for every row is **2026-10-26 (search date)**, not the date a live page was viewed.
- "not stated" = the source doesn't mention it. "not found" = the search didn't turn it up.

## The CPT problem affects all of these

[Inference] Even if a company accepts F-1 students, **you need CPT from USF** to work there in Summer 2027. The earlier report (`docs/research/transfer-decision-2026-10.md`) found:
- Since SEVP's August 24, 2026 guidance, many schools only approve CPT for training that is "required of all students in the program."
- No required internship was found for the USF AI major, and USF has not published a response.
- A court hearing on that guidance is set for November 6, 2026.

Ask USF ISSS whether a first-year AI student can get CPT for Summer 2027. Also ask about USF's "one full academic year" rule: by summer 2027 you'll have finished one year, which normally satisfies it. If CPT isn't available, events like Discover Citadel and Jane Street FTTP **may not need work authorization**, since they're short programs and not jobs. Confirm that with the organizer and ISSS.
